Leaf material tint reduced at runtime. CC0 permits redistribution in the game and source project.

## Scope and omissions
No Kenney models, White3d house, Allie models, paid models or Poly Haven models have been integrated. Only the three supplied Sketchfab GLBs and selected Quaternius assets above were used for the new environment. Existing 0.3 dog assets retain their earlier notices in assets/models and LICENSES.md.
Procedural physics boxes and shadow-only ceiling blocker are not presented as visible third-party architectural assets. The remaining map still contains the original prototype geometry.
